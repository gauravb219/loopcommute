Loop Commute Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Loop Commute is a trip-planning tool. It does not require an account and does not collect, sell or share personal information.

Data stored on your device

Your trip choices (cities, dates, travellers, itinerary edits) are saved only in your device's local storage so the app remembers them. They never leave your device. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes them.

Links to other services

Buttons open third-party sites such as MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Cleartrip, Skyscanner, Agoda, Booking.com, WhatsApp and Instagram. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Prices

Prices shown are estimates, not live quotes or offers.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions: write to the support email listed on our Google Play page.
